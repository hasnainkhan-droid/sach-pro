      Stay informed

      A few habits that stop a lot of scams.

      Useful guidance even before you run a check.

      Keep your codes private

      Never share an OTP, wallet PIN or card CVV in a call or chat. Fraudsters may pretend to represent a bank, BISP or a delivery company.

      Verify outside the message

      Do not trust a link, phone number or caller ID on sight. Use the official app or type a known website yourself to confirm a claim.

      Report suspected cybercrime

      Save evidence privately and follow the current official reporting process. NCCIA complaint portal ↗
